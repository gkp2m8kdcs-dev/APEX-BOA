Built for Investors Who Already Know Their Strategy

Every position, every trade, every dividend, and every receipt in one private record you control.

📊

Multi-Asset Ledger

Track stocks, ETFs, cryptocurrencies, bonds, real estate, commodities, and cash in a single weighted-average cost-basis ledger.

📝

Every Trade Captured

Log buys, sells, dividends, fees, deposits, withdrawals, and splits. Cost basis, average price, and unrealized P&L update the moment you save.

📷

Receipts Attached

Capture broker confirmations with the in-app camera or pull from your photo library. Receipts stay linked to the trade that produced them.

📈

Custom Charts

Equity curve, asset-class donut, monthly P&L bars, normalized benchmark, daily-return heatmap, winners-and-losers leaderboard, concentration meter.

⭐

Watchlist & Targets

Carry target prices and research notes. A green check seals every name that has crossed your target.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export the entire journal to a single JSON file and restore on any device with one tap. Your data, your control.
