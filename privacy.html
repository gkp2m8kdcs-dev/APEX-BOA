Privacy Policy

Your data stays on this device. No accounts. No servers. No tracking.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

APEX BOA is designed from the ground up as an offline-first personal journal. Nothing in this app talks to a server unless you explicitly export a backup file yourself.

1. Overview

APEX BOA ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal investment journal application for iOS. We respect your privacy and have built the app so that the data you record stays under your control. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, what it does not store, and what choices you have.

By using APEX BOA, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Store Locally On Your Device

All data you create in APEX BOA is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard on-device storage. The data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. Specifically, we store:

Holdings & Transactions

  • Holdings you add (symbol, name, asset class, notes, sector)
  • Transactions you record (date, type, quantity, price, fees, notes, tags)
  • Watchlist items and research notes
  • Receipt images you import through the camera or photo library
  • Display preferences (theme, currency, your chosen display name)

3. What We Never Do

APEX BOA does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. We never:

  • Sync to any cloud, server, or third-party service
  • Collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Contact your broker, bank, or wallet
  • Transmit any data over the network
  • Request notification, Face ID, contacts, or location permissions
  • Sell, rent, or trade your information
  • Use cookies, tracking pixels, or device fingerprinting

4. Permissions Requested by the App

APEX BOA requests only the iOS permissions necessary for the features you actively use:

Camera Permission

Used solely to capture a photo of a receipt. Images are stored on-device and attached to a transaction you choose. You may deny this permission and still use every other feature.

Photo Library Permission

Used solely when you choose to import an existing receipt from your photo library. Images are stored on-device and attached to a transaction you choose. You may deny this permission and still use every other feature.

We do not request permission for notifications, Face ID, contacts, location, microphone, or background tracking.

5. Your Control Over Your Data

Because your data lives on your device, you have full control at all times. You can:

  • Export a full backup as JSON from Settings → Export / Import
  • Restore from a previous JSON backup on any device
  • Reset to sample data (Settings → Reset) to replace your data with demo content
  • Erase all data (Settings → Erase) to permanently delete everything

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. If your device is lost, damaged, or wiped without a backup, the data cannot be recovered by us — because we never had it.

6. Third-Party Services

APEX BOA does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, social, or crash-reporting SDKs. The app contains no third-party trackers of any kind.

7. Children

APEX BOA is intended for adult users managing their own finances. The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

8. International Users

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is no cross-border data transfer to govern. If you choose to email us for support, your message is handled using a standard email provider governed by its own privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because the app does not contact you, we encourage you to review this page periodically.
