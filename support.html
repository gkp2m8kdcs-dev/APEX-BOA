Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All APEX BOA data is stored locally on your iPhone using standard iOS on-device storage. We do not maintain any servers, accounts, or cloud sync. If you uninstall the app or wipe your device without exporting first, the data cannot be recovered.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings → Export / Import and tap Export. APEX BOA will generate a single JSON file containing every holding, transaction, watchlist item, note, and receipt. Save that file to your Files app, iCloud Drive, or any other location you trust.

How do I restore from a backup?

Open Settings → Export / Import, tap Import, and select the previously exported JSON file. APEX BOA will merge the backup into the current journal.

Why aren't live prices showing?

APEX BOA is an offline-first journal. Live market data feeds are not part of the core experience and may not be available in your region or at all times. All price values you see are intended for reference and journaling — they are not investment advice. To record a price you observed, simply edit the transaction or holding with the price you saw.

How is cost basis calculated?

APEX BOA uses a weighted-average cost basis. Buys add to your quantity and total cost. Sells reduce quantity at the current average and realize gain or loss. Dividends and fees adjust cost basis per share. This is intended as a journal-grade approximation — verify all figures with your broker statements before filing taxes.

Does APEX BOA connect to my broker?

No. APEX BOA does not connect to any brokerage, exchange, or wallet. It does not place, route, or execute trades. It does not custody any of your assets. You remain solely responsible for all transactions recorded in the journal.

What permissions does the app request?

The app only requests the iOS permissions you actively use:

  • Camera — only when you choose to capture a receipt
  • Photo Library — only when you choose to import an existing receipt

No notification, Face ID, contacts, or location permissions are requested.

How do I erase all my data?

Open Settings → Erase and confirm. This permanently deletes every holding, transaction, watchlist item, note, and receipt on the device. This action cannot be undone — export a backup first if you want to keep anything.

Is APEX BOA investment advice?

No. APEX BOA is a personal journal and analytical companion. Nothing in the app constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The disclaimer is presented during onboarding and is reachable any time from Settings → Disclaimer.

Quick Tips

📸 Receipt Tips

Photograph receipts in good lighting on a flat surface. Use the "Attach to Trade" flow immediately after logging a transaction.

🎯 Watchlist Targets

Set target prices on watchlist items. A green check seals every name that has crossed your target — your private shortlist.

📊 Chart Filters

Tap any chart to switch time ranges. The Insights tab lets you isolate top holdings, sectors, or asset classes.

💾 Backup Habit

Export a JSON backup monthly. Email it to yourself or save it to iCloud Drive — you control where it lives.
